---
title: "Campaign report"
description: "Funnel, rates, timings, clicks and failure reasons for a campaign."
openapi: "GET /v1/campaigns/{id}/report"
---

- **`timing`**: average and median seconds to deliver, read and click, measured from when
  Truecaller accepted each message.
- **`clicks.by_button`**: taps per button and on links in the text. Taps on link buttons and quick
  replies are reported by Truecaller; links are counted through a short redirect when
  `track_links` is on.
- **`categories`**: failed and skipped messages grouped by reason. See
  [Errors](/api-reference/truecaller/errors).
