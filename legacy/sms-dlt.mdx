## Managing Entity IDs (Aura platform)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Entity IDs">
    Click **Entity IDs** in the navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an action">
    Select **Show Entity ID** to view existing entries, or **+ Add Entity ID** to create one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in details">
    In the dialog, provide the organization's **Entity ID** and **Entity Name**, then click
    **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit or update">
    Use the edit icon in the **Action** column to modify an entry, then click **Update**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate or deactivate">
    Use the toggle button to enable (active for SMS transmission) or disable an Entity ID.
  </Step>
</Steps>
