## What V1 covered

The legacy `docs.onextel.com` site documented three product areas: SMS (the "Aura" platform),
WhatsApp APIs, and RCS APIs. Voice and Email were listed in the legacy nav as "coming soon"
and were never published — no content exists for them.

The legacy SMS "Aura" section combined end-user portal instructions (campaigns, phonebook,
credits, sub-accounts, reports) with developer-facing material (API key generation, send/report
APIs, DLT entity/template/sender-ID registration, and marketing-platform plugins such as
MoEngage, WebEngage, LeadSquared, CleverTap, Zoho CRM, Salesforce, and Shopify). The legacy
WhatsApp and RCS sections documented a separate messaging API surface hosted on the `365cx.io`
domain (token-based auth, template-based message sends, delivery callbacks), which is distinct
from the current OnexAura Omni API.
