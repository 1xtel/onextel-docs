A reference for engineers integrating a voice platform. For the end-to-end picture, see
[How it works](/guides/whatsapp-voice/how-it-works).

## Signalling

| Leg | Protocol | Detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meta to the OneXtel gateway | SIP over TLS | TCP port 5061, TLS 1.2 or later. The certificate matches the gateway hostname. |
| Authentication of calls | SIP digest | The gateway challenges each call. Meta answers with credentials generated for your number. |
| OneXtel gateway to your platform | SIP over UDP, TCP or TLS | Your choice per trunk. Authenticated by source IP address. |

The gateway stays in the signalling path for the whole call, so BYE and re-INVITE reach both sides.

## Audio

| Leg | Transport | Encryption | Codecs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WhatsApp (Meta) side | RTP | SRTP, keys exchanged in the SDP (SDES) | Opus, 48 kHz, 20 ms packets |
| Your platform's side | RTP | Plain RTP, or SRTP with SDES keys, per trunk | G.711 µ-law (PCMU), G.711 A-law (PCMA) or Opus, per trunk |

WhatsApp uses Opus. When your trunk does not accept Opus, the gateway converts between Opus and G.711
for you. If your trunk lists Opus as a codec, the gateway can offer it to your platform directly.

<Info>
Audio is carried in both directions through the gateway, which holds the audio address that Meta and your
platform send to. This keeps NAT and firewall rules on your side simple: allow the gateway.
</Info>

### Who sends first

Meta expects the business side to send the first audio packet on every call. Make sure your platform
starts sending audio as soon as it answers.

## Keypad tones (DTMF)

Customers can open a keypad during a WhatsApp call. Meta sends the tones as standard RFC 4733
telephone-event packets in the audio stream, with an 8,000 Hz clock rate. Meta provides no separate
notification of the digits.

<Warning>
Passing these tones through to your platform is being validated. Confirm with your OneXtel account team
before you design a menu that depends on keypad input.
</Warning>

## Security

| Area | How it is protected |
| --- | --- |
| Signalling | TLS 1.2 or later between Meta and the gateway. |
| Who can call the gateway | Only networks that belong to Meta are accepted, and calls must pass digest authentication. |
| WhatsApp audio | SRTP. |
| Your trunk | Accepts calls only from the gateway's addresses, which you allow in your firewall. |
| Credentials | The credentials Meta generates for your number are stored encrypted by OneXtel and are never shown. |

## Limits and behaviour

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Direction | Inbound only. |
| Devices | WhatsApp on iPhone and Android. Meta does not support calls from WhatsApp on the web or desktop. |
| Cost | Meta does not charge for calls that customers place to your business. |
| Recording | None. Record on your own platform. |
| Hosting | The gateway runs in India. |
