Some telephony partners and contact centres accept SIP only over a private connection. For them, the OneXtel
gateway can reach your trunk through a site-to-site IPsec tunnel. The call signalling and the audio for that trunk
then travel only inside the tunnel. Calls between Meta and the gateway are not affected.

<Info>
Only OneXtel administrators create and change tunnels. You agree the settings with your account team and configure
your own gateway to match.
</Info>

## How tunnels and trunks fit together

- **One tunnel per partner gateway.** A tunnel connects the OneXtel gateway to one IPsec gateway on your side.
  Several trunks can use the same tunnel.
- **A trunk opts in.** Each trunk is set to reach its host either over the internet (the default) or through a
  named tunnel. Trunks that do not use a tunnel are unchanged.
- **The trunk host is a private address.** A trunk that goes through a tunnel must point at an IP address inside
  your tunnel subnets, and use UDP or TCP. TLS is for trunks reached over the internet.
- **Fallback still works.** A number can have a tunnel trunk first and an internet trunk as its backup, or the
  other way round.

## What you give OneXtel

| Item | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Your gateway's public IP address | The address your IPsec gateway uses for IKE. |
| Your subnets (your encryption domain) | One or more ranges, for example `10.20.30.0/24` or `10.20.31.5/32`, up to 16. They must not overlap `172.31.0.0/16`, which OneXtel uses internally. |
| Your IKE identity | Only if it is not your gateway's public IP. Most gateways use the IP. |
| IKE version | IKEv2 (the default) or IKEv1 in main mode. |
| Phase 1 (IKE) proposal | Encryption, integrity and Diffie-Hellman group, written as `aes256-sha256-modp2048`. The default is `aes256-sha256-modp2048`. Separate alternatives with commas. |
| Phase 2 (IPsec, ESP) proposal | Same format. Include a Diffie-Hellman group for perfect forward secrecy (`aes256-sha256-modp2048`), or leave it out for none (`aes256-sha256`). |
| Phase 1 lifetime | 600 to 86,400 seconds. The default is 28,800 (8 hours). |
| Phase 2 lifetime | 300 to 86,400 seconds. The default is 3,600 (1 hour). |
| Pre-shared key | 16 to 128 printable characters, without spaces, quotes or backslashes. |
| The trunk's private address | The IP address and port of your SIP platform inside your subnets, and UDP or TCP. |

<Warning>
Share the pre-shared key by phone or through a password manager, never by email or chat. OneXtel stores it
encrypted and never shows it again, so keep your own copy. To change it, agree a new key and OneXtel replaces the
old one.
</Warning>

## What OneXtel gives you

OneXtel gives you these details for your side of the tunnel:

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| OneXtel IPsec peer | The gateway's public IP address. |
| OneXtel IKE identity | The same public IP address. |
| OneXtel encryption domain | That IP address as a single host (`/32`). |
| Traffic inside the tunnel | SIP on UDP and TCP port 5060, and RTP audio on UDP ports 30000 to 30999. |

Allow SIP and RTP from the OneXtel encryption domain to your SIP platform inside the tunnel. Calls and audio come
from that address, not from the gateway addresses used for internet trunks.

<Note>
The OneXtel side of the tunnel is behind NAT, so the tunnel uses NAT traversal: IKE on UDP 500 and 4500, and the
encrypted traffic inside UDP 4500. Your gateway must accept NAT-T from the OneXtel peer.
</Note>

## Set up a tunnel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Agree the settings">
    Send your account team the details in [What you give OneXtel](#what-you-give-onextel), and take the details in
    [What OneXtel gives you](#what-onextel-gives-you). Share the pre-shared key out of band.
  </Step>
  <Step title="OneXtel opens its firewall for your gateway">
    OneXtel allows IKE and NAT-T from your gateway's public IP. Until this is done, your gateway's IKE messages do
    not reach the OneXtel gateway.
  </Step>
  <Step title="OneXtel creates the tunnel">
    OneXtel enters your settings and the pre-shared key. The OneXtel gateway starts the tunnel itself and keeps
    retrying until your side answers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your gateway">
    Set up the matching tunnel on your IPsec gateway: the same IKE version, proposals, lifetimes and key, with the
    OneXtel encryption domain as the remote side and your subnets as the local side.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that the tunnel is up">
    OneXtel confirms the tunnel shows as up. It usually comes up within a few seconds of both sides being ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Point the trunk at the tunnel">
    OneXtel sets the trunk's **Reach the host** to **Through tunnel** with your tunnel's name, and its host to your
    SIP platform's private address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place a test call">
    Call the WhatsApp number and confirm audio flows both ways, as in
    [Test your first call](/guides/whatsapp-voice/getting-started#test-your-first-call).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The gateway also sends its 30-second [health check](/guides/whatsapp-voice/connect-your-voice-platform#trunk-health)
to a tunnel trunk through the tunnel, so the trunk's health shows whether SIP gets through end to end.

## Tunnel states

OneXtel sees each tunnel's state in the console. The gateway checks it every few seconds.

| State | Meaning | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up | Both phases are established. OneXtel also sees the traffic in each direction. | Nothing. If calls still fail, check the trunk's private address and your firewall inside the tunnel. |
| Connecting, IKE up but no IPsec SA | Phase 1 succeeded, phase 2 did not. | The subnets on both sides (your subnets, and the OneXtel `/32`) and the phase 2 proposal, including whether both sides use PFS with the same group. |
| Connecting, IKE not established | An IKE session has started but phase 1 is not complete. | The peer address, the IKE identities, the pre-shared key and the phase 1 proposal. |
| Down | No IKE session: your gateway is not answering on UDP 500 or 4500, or rejects the proposal. | That your gateway is reachable from the OneXtel peer, that NAT-T is allowed, that OneXtel has opened its firewall for your gateway's IP, and that the IP is correct. |
| No report | The gateway has not reported the tunnel's state for over a minute. | Nothing on your side. OneXtel checks its own gateway. |

A tunnel with no pre-shared key, or one that OneXtel has disabled, is not started.
