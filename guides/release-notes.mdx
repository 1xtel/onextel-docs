<Update label="16 November 2026" description="MoEngage: WhatsApp to RCS fallback" tags={["WhatsApp", "RCS", "Connectors"]}>
  MoEngage WhatsApp campaigns can now fall back to RCS. If a WhatsApp message fails, or no
  delivery report arrives within the wait time you set, OneXtel sends an RCS message instead.

  - **Set once per WhatsApp sender.** Every campaign on that sender gets the fallback.
  - **RCS templates are created for you.** Add your RCS bot ID to the sender, and the connector
    creates an RCS template for each WhatsApp template, with matching variables.
  - **One outcome in MoEngage.** When the RCS message delivers, MoEngage shows the message as
    delivered, and the WhatsApp failure is never shown.

  [Add RCS fallback to a MoEngage sender](/plugins/moengage#add-rcs-fallback-optional)
</Update>

<Update label="11 November 2026" description="CleverTap: WhatsApp, with RCS and SMS fallback" tags={["WhatsApp", "RCS", "SMS", "Connectors"]}>
  CleverTap campaigns can now send WhatsApp through OneXtel, using CleverTap's Generic WhatsApp
  provider.

  - **Templates and freeform messages** from your OneXtel WhatsApp Business Account, with your
    approved templates imported into CleverTap.
  - **Optional fallback.** A WhatsApp message that fails, or isn't delivered within the wait time,
    is sent over RCS, SMS or both, in the order your Fallback ID sets.
  - **Delivery reports, inbound replies and clicks** on OneXtel-tracked links reach CleverTap.

  [Set up WhatsApp in CleverTap](/plugins/clevertap#setup-whatsapp)
</Update>
