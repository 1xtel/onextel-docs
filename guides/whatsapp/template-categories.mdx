description: "How Meta categorizes WhatsApp message templates into marketing, utility, and authentication, effective July 1, 2025."
---

Meta's template category guidelines define how every WhatsApp message template is
categorized. The category you choose is validated against these guidelines when you
submit a template for approval, and it determines how the template is priced and
what content is allowed in it.

<Info>
  These guidelines are effective **July 1, 2025**.
</Info>

## The three categories

Message templates can be categorized as:

- **Marketing** — Enable businesses to achieve a wide range of goals, from
  generating awareness to driving sales and retargeting customers. Examples can
  include new product/service/feature announcements; targeted promotions/offers;
  or cart abandonment reminders.
- **Utility** — Enable businesses to send non-promotional messages that are either
  specific to and requested by the user or essential or critical to the user.
  Examples can include order-related updates, payment reminders, or fraud
  awareness messages.
- **Authentication** — Enable businesses to verify a user's identity, potentially
  at various steps of the customer journey.

<Warning>
  Businesses can only send template messages to users who have subscribed to
  receive messages from them on WhatsApp.
</Warning>

## Common categorization pitfalls

Meta applies two default rules that frequently surprise template authors. Both
result in a template being categorized as **marketing** even when you submitted
it as utility:

| Situation | Resulting category |
| --- | --- |
| **Mixed content** — the template contains both utility and marketing content (e.g. an order update with a promo/offer) | Marketing |
| **Unclear content** — the template contents give no context (e.g. contents are only `{{1}}` or "Congratulations!") | Marketing |

<Note>
  The example templates in these guides are illustrative only. Templates
  containing similar content, or content including the example text, may be
  categorized differently based on their exact content.
</Note>
