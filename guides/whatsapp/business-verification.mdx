---
title: "Business verification"
description: "Get your Meta business portfolio verified through OneXtel, to raise your messaging limits and show your verified business name."
---

Meta verification confirms that your business is who it says it is. A verified business portfolio
gets higher messaging limits and can show its verified business name. Through the portal, you
submit your documents and OneXtel passes them to Meta as your partner — Meta calls this
[Partner-led Business Verification](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/solution-providers/partner-led-business-verification).

<Note>
The **Business Portfolio** tab appears only after your tenant administrator switches on Partner
Led Business Verification for your account.
</Note>

## Submit a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Business Portfolio">
    Go to **Channels → WhatsApp → Asset Management → Business Portfolio** and click **Request
    Verification**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the portfolio ID">
    Enter the **BM ID** — your Meta business portfolio (Business Manager) ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your documents">
    Upload up to 3 files that show your legal business name and address — for example a
    certificate of incorporation, a GST certificate or a utility bill.

    | Requirement | Value |
    | --- | --- |
    | File types | PDF, JPG, JPEG or PNG |
    | Size | Up to 5 MB each |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit for Verification**. The request goes straight to Meta.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Meta allows three submissions per business.** Check that the documents are clear, current and
show the same legal name and address as your business portfolio before you submit. After three
rejections you can't use this route again.
</Warning>

## Track the request

The list shows **Business Portfolio ID**, **Business Portfolio Name**, **Submission Time**,
**Verification Attempts**, **Verification Status**, **Rejection Reason**, **Last Update Time** and
**Submission ID**. Click **Sync** for the latest status from Meta.

If a request is rejected, read the **Rejection Reason**, fix the documents, and submit again.
