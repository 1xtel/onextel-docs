The Campaign feature on the OneXtel portal creates, manages, and sends bulk or targeted messages across multiple channels, including WhatsApp and RCS. Use it to promote offers, share updates, and deliver notifications efficiently.

- **WhatsApp Campaigns** — send approved message templates, images, and documents through the verified WhatsApp Business API.
- **RCS Campaigns** — deliver rich media, interactive buttons, and branded layouts to users' native messaging apps (covered in the RCS Campaign guide).

Each campaign includes customization, scheduling, and analytics to track delivery, read rates, and overall engagement.

## Open the campaigns dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Channels">
    In the left pane of the platform, click **Channels**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a channel">
    Select the **WhatsApp** channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Campaigns">
    Click **Campaigns**. The campaigns dashboard (**View Campaign** page) appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the dashboard shows

The campaigns dashboard displays all campaigns created for the WhatsApp and RCS channels as cards.
Each card shows the created date, channel, status, campaign name, template, uploaded file, number
of records, creator and scheduled time. A `Stopped` campaign also shows its **Failure Reason**.

## Find a campaign

- **Search** by campaign name or campaign ID.
- **Filter** by Channel, Template, Username, Status and Date. **Clear all** resets the filters.
- **Refresh** reloads the list with the latest statuses.

The list is paginated; use **Prev** and **Next** at the bottom of the page to move between pages.

## Campaign statuses and actions

The buttons on each card depend on the campaign's status.

| Status | Actions |
| --- | --- |
| `Created` / `Draft` (not launched) | Edit, Clone, Delete |
| `Scheduled` | View, Clone, Reschedule, Cancel |
| `Running` | View, Clone, Pause, Stop |
| `Paused` | View, Clone, Resume, Stop |
| `Completed` / `Stopped` | View, Clone |
| `Cancelled` / `Failed` | View, Clone |
| `Pending` / `Rejected` (maker-checker) | Edit, View, Clone |
| `Approved` (maker-checker) | Edit, View, Clone, Launch (checker only) |

**View** opens a read-only copy of the campaign set-up: recipients, sender, variable mapping,
settings, batching and schedule. It doesn't show results — use
[WhatsApp reports](/guides/whatsapp/reports) for those.

<Note>
The `Pending`, `Rejected` and `Approved` statuses appear only when maker-checker is switched on for
your account. A maker's campaign goes to `Pending`; a checker approves or rejects it, and launches
it once approved. See [Roles](/guides/platform/roles#campaign-maker-checker).
</Note>

## Start or copy a campaign

- **Clone Campaign** — duplicate an existing campaign instead of rebuilding it.
- **New Campaign** — click **+ New Campaign** in the top right to start a new campaign. See
  [Create a WhatsApp campaign](/guides/whatsapp/campaign-create).
