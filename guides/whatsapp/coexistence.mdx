title: "WhatsApp Coexistence"
description: "Run one WhatsApp number on the WhatsApp Business app and on OneXtel at the same time, and what Meta allows on a Coexistence number."
---

Coexistence is a Meta capability that runs one WhatsApp number on the WhatsApp Business app and
on the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) at the same time. You keep using the app for
one-to-one conversations, and connect the same number to OneXtel for campaigns and automation.

The number is connected through Meta's Embedded Signup flow for Business app users. During
sign-up you verify the number, approve data sharing, and can sync your existing contacts and
recent chat history. To connect a number, see
[Keep using the WhatsApp Business app](/guides/whatsapp/phone-numbers#keep-using-the-whatsapp-business-app-coexistence).

## What you can do on a Coexistence number

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Enterprise customers">
    OneXtel connects a number that's already in use on the WhatsApp Business app. The app keeps
    working alongside the OneXtel portal, and on the number you can:

    - run outbound campaigns and broadcasts,
    - automate conversations with the chatbot module,
    - hand conversations to human agents with the live agent tools.

    The WhatsApp Business app and the OneXtel portal run as two separate systems on the same
    number. They don't share one inbox, so each is managed on its own.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Technology partners">
    Technology partners connect their own end customers through Embedded Signup and use Meta's
    WhatsApp Business Platform APIs directly through OneXtel:

    - self-serve onboarding with Embedded Signup,
    - full conversation and messaging API access,
    - every Coexistence feature Meta supports.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What Meta allows

These limits come from Meta's Coexistence framework and apply to every Coexistence number,
whichever provider it's connected through. Meta lists them under
[limitations](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/embedded-signup/onboarding-business-app-users#limitations).

| Capability | Available | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-to-one messaging in the Business app | Yes | Send and receive individual messages in the app as before. |
| Campaigns and broadcasts through the API | Yes | Run outbound campaigns on the number from the OneXtel portal. |
| Chatbot | Yes | Automated conversation flows run on the number. |
| Live agent | Yes | Agents manage conversations with the OneXtel live agent tools. |
| Contact and history sync | Once | Contacts and up to about 6 months of chat history sync once, within 24 hours of onboarding. |
| Throughput | Limited | Capped at a low messages-per-second rate, well below an API-only number. |
| Re-syncing history or contacts | No | Sync happens once per onboarding. Miss the 24-hour window and the number has to be offboarded and onboarded again. |
| Group chats, voice and video calls | No | Not synced or supported through the API. |
| Native broadcast lists | No | Existing lists become read-only, and new ones can't be created. |
| Catalogues, orders and status updates | App only | Available in the Business app, not through the API. |
| Business profile edits (name, address, website) | App only | Edit them in the Business app. |
| Disappearing messages, view once and live location | No | Switched off for one-to-one chats once Coexistence is active. |

<Warning>
Contact and history sync happens once, within 24 hours of onboarding. If you miss it, the only
way to sync is to offboard the number and onboard it again.
</Warning>
