---
title: "Add OneXtel to your number"
description: "Connect OneXtel to a WhatsApp number you already use with another provider, then set conversation routing so OneXtel receives incoming messages."
---

If your WhatsApp number already works with another provider, you don't move it. You share your
existing WhatsApp account with OneXtel instead. The number stays where it is, conversations
aren't interrupted, and your previous provider keeps working until you remove them.

This replaces the old way of moving a number between providers, which meant offboarding,
switching off two-step verification and verifying the number again. Meta no longer supports
moving a number between providers. For background, see
[WhatsApp account model](/guides/whatsapp/account-model).

## Before you start

- You need an **admin** of your own Meta business portfolio. Only an admin can complete the
  sign-up and change conversation routing.
- Have your OneXtel portal account to hand.

## Connect OneXtel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start Embedded Signup">
    In the OneXtel portal, go to **Channels → WhatsApp → Asset Management → Phone Numbers** and
    click **Add WABA**. Meta's sign-up window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your existing number">
    On the first screen, select the phone number you already use. The **Business Portfolio** and
    **WhatsApp account** fields are greyed out. That's expected: Meta fills them in from the
    number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the sign-up">
    Complete the remaining screens. Meta creates a new Messaging account for OneXtel on your
    number, billed through OneXtel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set conversation routing">
    Route conversations to OneXtel, as described below, before you test incoming messages.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set conversation routing

When several providers share a number, Meta's **conversation routing** decides which one
receives and answers each conversation. After you add OneXtel, your previous provider stays in
charge until you change the setting. Only your business can change it; OneXtel can't change it
for you.

Until routing points to OneXtel:

- incoming messages and message webhooks don't reach OneXtel,
- Meta's health status says *Your app is not subscribed to the message webhook*, even though the
  webhook URLs are set,
- free-form replies sent through OneXtel fail with error `131070`: *Message failed to send
  because another app is controlling this thread now*.

To route conversations to OneXtel, a business portfolio admin does this in Meta Business Suite:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your WhatsApp account">
    Go to **Business settings → WhatsApp accounts** and select the account or number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open conversation routing">
    Open the **Conversation routing** tab and click **Manage**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose OneXtel everywhere">
    Select **Onextel Limited**, the name OneXtel is registered under with Meta, in every
    dropdown: the five message routing types and **Thread control – All conversations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Finish**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Set routing straight after onboarding. If OneXtel isn't receiving any messages on a shared
number, check routing first.
</Tip>

## Bring your templates

Templates aren't copied to OneXtel's new Messaging account. If you need the templates you used
with your previous provider, OneXtel can copy them from that provider's Messaging account on
request. Your number can have several Messaging accounts, each with different templates, so tell
OneXtel the WABA ID (Messaging account ID) to copy from. Email
[support@onextel.com](mailto:support@onextel.com).

Meta resets the quality rating and messaging tier of copied templates to baseline, so expect a
ramp-up of about 2 to 4 weeks. Meta describes this in
[template migration](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/account-model-evolution/).

## Billing

Messages OneXtel sends are charged to OneXtel's Messaging account on your number. Messages your
other provider sends are charged to theirs, whichever provider is handling the conversation. All
providers share the number's throughput and your portfolio's messaging limits. See
[What changes on a shared number](/guides/whatsapp/account-model#what-changes-on-a-shared-number).
