Meta is adding usernames to WhatsApp, for businesses and for customers. Both are optional, and
phone numbers keep working as before.

| | Business username | Customer username |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claimed by | A business | A WhatsApp user |
| Linked to | The business phone number | The user's personal account |
| Example | `@onextel` | `@john123` |
| What it's for | Branding, and helping customers find the business | Privacy: the customer can chat without showing their number |

## Reserve your business username

Business usernames are globally unique and first come, first served. Your WhatsApp Business phone
number stays the same.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your phone numbers">
    In Meta Business Manager, open **WhatsApp Manager** and go to the **Phone numbers** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the profile">
    Click the settings icon for the number and open the **Profile** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the username">
    Under **Username**, click **Create**. Enter a username or pick a suggested one, and click
    **Save**. You can edit it later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For help reserving a username, email [support@onextel.com](mailto:support@onextel.com).

## BSUID

The BSUID (business-scoped user ID) is a customer ID that Meta generates for each business
portfolio. It isn't visible to customers and appears only in APIs and webhooks. Use it to
identify a customer who chats with you by username instead of phone number. If you manage
several business portfolios, use the **Parent BSUID** across them.

### When Meta sends a BSUID instead of a phone number

Meta sends the customer's username and BSUID, without a phone number, only when **all** of these
are true:

- The customer has adopted a WhatsApp username.
- The customer is contacting your business for the first time.
- The customer hasn't shared their phone number.
- The customer isn't in your business contacts.
- There's been no interaction in the last 30 days.

In every other case you get the phone number as usual.

### Where BSUIDs appear

- **Campaigns**: choose **BSUID** as the recipient type to send to customers by BSUID. See
  [Create a campaign](/guides/campaigns/create-a-campaign#add-recipients).
- **Reports**: WhatsApp reports have BSUID, Parent BSUID and Customer Username columns. See
  [WhatsApp reports](/guides/whatsapp/reports).
- **Callbacks**: WhatsApp callbacks carry the BSUID, Parent BSUID and username when Meta sends
  them. See [Callback configuration](/guides/platform/callback-configuration).
