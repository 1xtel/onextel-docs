---
title: "Create WhatsApp templates"
description: "Build a WhatsApp template in the OneXtel portal, submit it to Meta for approval, and manage it once it is live."
---

WhatsApp needs an approved template for every message you start: offers, alerts, reminders and
one-time passcodes. You build the template in the portal, the portal submits it to Meta, and Meta
approves or rejects it. Replies inside the 24-hour customer service window don't need a template.

<Note>
Pick the category before you write. Meta prices by category and rejects a template whose content
doesn't match it — see [Template categories](/guides/whatsapp/template-categories).
</Note>

## Create a template

Go to **Channels → WhatsApp → Asset Management → Templates** and click **Create Template**. The
builder has three steps: **Set up template**, **Edit template** and **Submit for review**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the approval scope">
    - **Individual** submits the template to one WhatsApp Business Account (WABA). Pick the
      **WABA ID**.
    - **Pool** submits the same template to every WABA in one of your
      [number pools](/guides/whatsapp/phone-numbers#number-pools) in one go, so it is approved
      for all of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the category and type">
    | Category | Types | Use it for |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Marketing | Custom, Catalog, Flows, Order Details, Limited-Time Offer, Carousel (up to 10 cards) | Offers, launches, newsletters, re-engagement |
    | Utility | Custom, Flows, Order Status, Order Details | Order updates, bills, reminders, account alerts |
    | Authentication | One-time Passcode | OTPs for login or transactions |

    Click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the template and pick the language">
    The **Template name** takes lowercase letters, numbers and underscores only — for example
    `order_shipped` — up to 512 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the variable type">
    **Number** gives `{{1}}`, `{{2}}` and so on. **Name** gives named variables such as
    `{{first_name}}`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the content">
    | Part | Rules |
    | --- | --- |
    | Header (optional) | None, Text, Image, Video, GIF, Document or Location |
    | Body (required) | Up to 1,024 characters. Click **Add variable** for the personal parts, and give a sample value for each variable — Meta reviews the samples |
    | Footer (optional) | Up to 60 characters |
    | Buttons (optional) | Up to 10. More than 3 show to the customer as a list |

    Button types:

    | Group | Button | Limit |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Quick reply | Custom | — |
    | Call to action | Visit website | 2 |
    | Call to action | Call phone number | 1 |
    | Call to action | Complete Flow | 1 |
    | Call to action | Copy offer code | 1 |
    | Call to action | Share Contact Info | 1 |

    Tick **Link tracking** to opt the template out of Meta's URL click tracking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the message validity period">
    Tick **Set custom validity period** to choose how long WhatsApp keeps trying to deliver. A
    message that isn't delivered in that time expires and isn't charged.

    | Category | Allowed range |
    | --- | --- |
    | Authentication | 30 seconds to 15 minutes |
    | Utility | 30 seconds to 12 hours |
    | Marketing | 12 hours to 30 days |

    Without a custom value, WhatsApp's standard validity applies, and the builder shows it.

    <Warning>
    Set a validity period on every template you use with
    [fallback](/guides/omnichannel/fallback-overview). WhatsApp can't take a time-to-live on
    each message, so without one the customer can get the WhatsApp message late *and* the
    fallback message.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the preview and submit">
    Check the **Template Preview** on the right, then click **Submit for Review**. The status
    moves from `Pending` to `Approved` or `Rejected`. Meta usually decides within minutes,
    sometimes a few hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Get approved first time

- Pick the category that matches the content. A promotion submitted as Utility is rejected.
- Don't start or end the body with a variable, and keep variables to the minimum.
- Give realistic sample values.

Meta's full rules are in its
[template guidelines](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/overview).

## Manage templates

The template list shows **Template Name**, **Language**, **Category**, **Quality Rating** and
**Status**. Use **Filter** to search by name, language, category, quality rating or status, and
**Sync** to pull the latest statuses from Meta.

Each template's **⋮** menu has:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Edit | Change an approved or rejected template. It goes back to Meta for review |
| Clone | Copy it as a starting point for a new template |
| Preview | See how it looks on a phone |
| Generate cURL | Show a ready-made API request for this template, for your developers to copy |
| Delete | Remove the template |

<Note>
Meta can change an approved template's category after reviewing how it is used — most often from
Utility to Marketing. If your tenant administrator has switched on **Category Blocking**, sending
stops for a template whose category changed, so you aren't charged the higher rate without
knowing. Review the template and create a new one in the right category. See
[WhatsApp settings](/guides/whatsapp/settings).
</Note>
