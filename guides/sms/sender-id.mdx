---
title: "Sender IDs"
description: "Add, view, disable, and delete Sender IDs from the OneXtel platform."
---

A Sender ID identifies the message origin displayed to recipients. It enables brand or service recognition for messages, and the platform uses it to route and deliver messages correctly. Sender ID approval ensures compliance and prevents message rejection.

Sender IDs are managed from the **Sender IDs** tab under the **Templates** menu.

## Add a Sender ID

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sender IDs">
    In the left pane, click **Templates**, then click the **Sender IDs** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Sender ID">
    Click **+Add Sender ID**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Sender ID">
    In the pop-up, enter the Sender ID. Keep it between 3 and 15 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set as default (optional)">
    Select the **Set as Default** checkbox to make this Sender ID the default for templates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add**. The platform adds the Sender ID.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View Sender IDs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sender IDs">
    In the left pane, click **Templates**, then click the **Sender IDs** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show Sender IDs">
    Click **Show Sender ID**. The platform displays the Sender IDs and their details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Disable a Sender ID

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Sender ID list">
    In the left pane, click **Templates**, then click the **Sender IDs** tab and click **Show Sender ID** to display the Sender IDs and their details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle off">
    In the **Action** column, deselect the toggle to disable the Sender ID.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete a Sender ID

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Sender ID list">
    In the left pane, click **Templates**, then click the **Sender IDs** tab and click **Show Sender ID**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete">
    In the **Action** column, click the **Delete** icon.
  </Step>
</Steps>
