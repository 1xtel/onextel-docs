The **Additional Settings** section of the campaign creation form has three
optional features: **Short and Track URLs in Body Variables**, **Message
Expiry** and **Fallback**. Click **Additional Settings** in the campaign
form to expand it.

## Short and Track URLs in Body Variables

Shortens the links in the body variables and tracks every click, so clicks
appear in the URL views of the
[Summary Report](/guides/rcs-platform/reports-summary#url).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch it on">
    Turn on **Short and Track URLs in Body Variables**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the domain">
    Pick the **Domain** the short links use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which links">
    Choose **Short and Track all Urls**, or pick the specific body variables
    whose links should be shortened.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture location (optional)">
    Tick **Get Location** to record the location of each click.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Message Expiry

Sets when your RCS message should expire. Once expired, the message is revoked
and cannot be delivered. Expiry works on every operator, and overrides the
TTL saved on the template.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable message expiry">
    Select the **Message Expiry** checkbox. The **Message Expiry Settings**
    dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the expiry type">
    | Expiry type | Behavior |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Duration from send time (TTL)** | Message expires after a set duration from the time it's sent. |
    | **Absolute expiry time** | Message expires at a specific date and time. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the duration (TTL)">
    For TTL expiry, enter the expiry duration value in **Duration (TTL)**
    (e.g., `3.5s`, `30m`, `2h`, `1d`) and choose the unit of time from the
    **Time Unit** dropdown (Seconds, Minutes, Hours, Days).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Expiry Settings** to save the configured expiry settings, or
    **Cancel** to close the dialog without saving changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fallback

Fallback re-sends the message on another channel — WhatsApp or SMS — when
RCS fails or doesn't confirm delivery in time, for example on a handset
that can't receive RCS. Add recipients first, then:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Fallback">
    Turn on **Fallback**, choose a **Fallback Configuration**, and click
    **Apply Fallback**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the wait time">
    Enter the wait time, in seconds, before the first fallback starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the fallback message">
    Fill in the fields for each fallback channel. Values can be fixed text,
    file columns such as `@name`, or a mix — for example
    `Dear @name, your order has shipped`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

With fallback, the RCS message is given a TTL of 95% of the wait time, so it
expires before the fallback goes out and the customer doesn't get both.
Fallback configurations are created under **Channels > Fallback** — see
[Configure fallback](/guides/omnichannel/configure-fallback).
