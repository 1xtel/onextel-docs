The final sections of the campaign creation form control how and when messages
go out: **Batching**, **Repeat Campaign**, and **Schedule**.

## Batching

Batching controls how messages are sent to recipients — either all at once or
in smaller, scheduled groups (batches). Click **Batching** in the campaign
form to expand it.

The Batching Configuration panel shows:

| Section | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Recipients** | The total number of recipients who will receive the messages (e.g., 19,999). |
| **Method** | Three batching methods: All, Split, and Manual. |
| **Batches Preview** | A table of batch numbers and sizes for the chosen method. |

Select any one method and enter the details:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="All">
    Sends the message to all recipients in a single batch. Best suited for
    smaller campaigns or when all recipients should receive the message
    simultaneously.

    - By default, the **All** option is selected.
    - The system automatically creates one batch containing all recipients.

    Example: 19,999 recipients → Batch 1: 19,999 recipients.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Split">
    Automatically divides the total recipients into multiple smaller batches
    (maximum 10). Helps distribute large campaigns over intervals to avoid
    message congestion.

    1. Select the **Split** option.
    2. Enter the **Number of Batches** (e.g., 3).
    3. Set the **Interval Between Batches** (e.g., `00:30:00`).
    4. The system displays a preview of batches with the recipient count and
       schedule time.

    Example: Batch 1 – 6,667 (2:15 PM), Batch 2 – 6,666 (2:45 PM),
    Batch 3 – 6,666 (3:15 PM).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Manual">
    Allows manual configuration of batch size and schedule time. Useful for
    controlling how and when each batch is sent.

    1. Select the **Manual** option.
    2. Enter the **Batch Size** (e.g., 19,999).
    3. Set the **Schedule Time** manually for each batch.
    4. Click **+** to add more batches (if required).

    Example: Batch 1 – 19,999 recipients scheduled for Nov 7, 2025, 02:18 PM.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Repeat Campaign

Repeat Campaign allows scheduling recurring campaigns without recreating them
each time, ensuring messages are sent consistently based on the selected
frequency and duration.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Repeat Campaign">
    Click **Repeat Campaign** in the campaign form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable repetition">
    Select the **Enable Repeat** checkbox. The Frequency, Configuration,
    Ends, and Schedule Summary controls appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a frequency">
    Enter the following details as per the requirements of the campaign:

    | Frequency | Description | Configuration |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | **Daily** | Sends the campaign every day at the scheduled time. | Keep **Ends** as `Never` or choose an end date. The Schedule Summary shows "Every day". |
    | **Weekly** | Sends the campaign on selected days every other week or on alternate weeks. | Choose the interval (Every, Alternate, or After weeks), then select specific days (e.g., Mon, Wed, Fri). The Schedule Summary shows "Every Week". |
    | **Monthly** | Sends the campaign every month based on specific dates or days. | Choose the interval (Every, Alternate, or After months). Under **Repeat On**, select Specific Dates or Days of Week, then the required Days of Week and Week Position (e.g., First Monday). The Schedule Summary shows "Every Month". |
    | **Custom** | Allows you to manually select specific dates and times for the campaign. | Click **+ Add Date & Time** to schedule manually. The Schedule Summary shows "Custom — Add specific dates and times". |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Schedule Summary">
    Confirm the summary matches the intended recurrence before moving on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Schedule

The Schedule section automates the campaign launch timing based on business
needs. Click **Schedule** in the campaign form, then select the type of
Campaign Execution:

### Immediate Execution

Launches the campaign instantly as soon as it's ready. Useful for quick or
urgent sends.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Schedule">
    Click **Schedule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Immediate Execution">
    Select the **Immediate Execution** option. The panel shows the status
    (`READY TO LAUNCH`), the execution mode ("As soon as campaign is
    launched"), and the current local time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch">
    Click **Launch**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Scheduled Execution

Allows setting a specific date, time, and time zone for the campaign to start
automatically. Ideal for planned or time-sensitive campaigns.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Scheduled Execution">
    In the Schedule section, select the **Scheduled Execution** option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the time zone">
    Click the **Time Zone** dropdown and choose the preferred time zone
    (e.g., `Asia/Kolkata` — IST, India Standard Time) for accurate
    scheduling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date and time">
    Click the calendar icon or field under **Scheduled Date & Time** and
    select the desired date and time for campaign execution (e.g.,
    November 10, 2025, at 11:08 AM).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Schedule Summary">
    The **Schedule Summary** displays both local (e.g., IST) and UTC formats
    along with the selected time zone for confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch">
    Click **Launch**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The Schedule section also shows **Test** and **Draft** buttons alongside
  **Launch**. Campaigns saved as drafts appear on the campaigns dashboard with
  the Draft status.
</Note>
