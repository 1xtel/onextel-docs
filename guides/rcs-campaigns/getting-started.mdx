## Signing in

Sign in to the OneXtel portal with the URL and credentials supplied for your account — see
[Signing in](/guides/platform/getting-started).

<Tip>
  If you forget your password, use the **Forgot Password?** link below the
  password field on the sign-in page.
</Tip>

## Dashboard

The Dashboard provides a quick overview of activities and performance within
the platform. It tracks credits, message delivery statistics, and campaign
progress in real time.

### Main Dashboard

After accessing the URL and logging in, the OneXtel WhatsApp and RCS platform
displays the Main Dashboard, which includes:

| Panel | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Credits Balance** | The total available credits, displayed at the top right. |
| **Summary Overview** | Weekly and monthly reports for Submitted, Delivered, Failed, and Awaited messages. |
| **Today's Delivered Percent** | The percentage of messages delivered for the day. |
| **Quick Stats** | The number of messages sent today, this week, and this month. |
| **Scheduled SMS** | Lists the upcoming scheduled SMS. |

The left navigation pane gives access to the platform modules, including
**Dashboard**, **Campaign**, **User Management**, **Credits**, **Channels**,
**Reports**, **Phonebook**, and **Notification**.
