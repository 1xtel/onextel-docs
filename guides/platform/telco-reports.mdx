---
title: "Telco Reports"
description: "Compare SMS delivery performance across telecom operators and accounts to isolate where messages are failing."
---

Telco Reports summarises SMS performance broken down by account and sender ID type, showing how
much traffic was submitted, delivered, failed, and is still awaited.

Where the [dashboard](/guides/platform/getting-started#the-dashboard) tells you *that* delivery
is down, this is where you find out *where* — because a delivery problem confined to one
operator or one sender ID type is a different problem from one affecting everything.

## View the report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Telco Reports">
    In the left pane, click **Telco Reports**. The **Partner Summary** tab opens by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the period">
    Select the **From date** and **To date** range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the record limit">
    Set the **Limit** if you need more or fewer rows than the default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Click **Search** to display the report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export, if needed">
    Click **Download** to export the data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reading the report

Totals appear at the top, with a per-row breakdown by TUC and sender ID type beneath.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Submitted | Accepted by the platform and passed onward |
| Delivered | Confirmed as reaching the handset |
| Failed | Rejected or undeliverable |
| Awaited | Sent, with no final status returned yet |

<Note>
**Awaited** is not the same as failed. A message can sit awaited legitimately — the handset is
off, or the operator has not yet returned a receipt. Awaited counts that stay high over a long
date range, though, usually mean delivery receipts are not coming back at all, which is a
[callback configuration](/guides/platform/callback-configuration) problem rather than a
delivery one.
</Note>

Comparing Submitted against Delivered per row is the fastest way to spot a route or sender ID
that is underperforming while the account average still looks healthy.
