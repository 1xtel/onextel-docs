---
title: "All Schedule"
description: "See every scheduled message queued across an account, and cancel schedules before they send."
---

All Schedule lists every message queued to send later, across the whole account rather than one
campaign at a time. It is the single place to confirm what is about to go out — and the place to
stop it if something is wrong.

## View scheduled messages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open All Schedule">
    In the left pane, click **All Schedule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the account">
    On the **View All Schedule** tab, select the **TUC** from the dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Set a record **Limit** if needed, then click **Search**. Scheduled records for that account
    are listed. If nothing is queued, the view reports that no scheduled data was found.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="All Schedule">
  <img src="/images/platform/all-schedule.png" alt="All Schedule tab with account selector, limit, Search and Delete All" />

## Cancel schedules

Use **Delete All** to clear the scheduled records returned by your current search.

<Warning>
**Delete All** cancels every schedule matching the current filter, not just the visible rows,
and a cancelled schedule cannot be restored — it has to be rebuilt in its campaign. Confirm the
TUC selection before using it.
</Warning>

<Note>
Check this page before any large or time-sensitive send. A campaign scheduled twice, or one left
queued from a test run, is invisible from inside the individual campaign screens but obvious
here.
</Note>
