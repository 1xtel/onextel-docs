The OneXtel portal is where campaigns are built, templates are managed, reports are read, and
the account is administered. Everything below is available to any signed-in user, subject to
the [role](/guides/platform/roles) they have been given.

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the portal">
    Go to the OneXtel portal URL in a web browser. Your account manager provides this during
    onboarding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Enter the **Username** and **Password** for your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the terms">
    Select the **I accept the Terms and Conditions** checkbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Click **Sign In**. The dashboard appears once you are authenticated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Forgotten your password? Use **Forgot Password?** on the sign-in screen rather than asking an
administrator to reset it — credentials should never be shared.

<Note>
If two-factor authentication is enabled on your account, you are prompted for a second
verification step after your password. 2FA is switched on per account — see
[Platform overview](/guides/platform/overview).
</Note>

## The dashboard

The dashboard is the landing page after sign-in and gives a real-time view of message
performance.

| Panel | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Summary Overview | Submitted, delivered, failed, and awaited volumes, toggleable between **Week** and **Month** |
| Today's Delivered Percent | The proportion of today's traffic that reached recipients |
| Quick Stats | Volumes submitted today, this week, and this month |

Your available credit is shown in the header at all times, split into **Web** and **API** —
traffic sent from the portal and traffic sent programmatically are metered separately. See
[Credits](/guides/platform/credits).

<Note>
**Submitted** counts messages the platform accepted, not messages that arrived. The gap between
Submitted and Delivered is where delivery problems show up first — if it widens, check the
delivery reports before assuming a send succeeded.
</Note>

## What you can do

A TUC account manages day-to-day messaging operations under a tenant. From the portal you can:

- **Send messages** — one-off, dynamic, or campaign sends, via web, API, or SMPP
- **Create and manage templates** for each channel
- **Manage users** within your account's scope, and assign their roles
- **Generate reports** on delivery, sender IDs, campaigns, templates, and click data
- **Allocate credit** to users and sub-accounts
- **Maintain the phonebook** of contacts and groups
- **Configure blacklisted numbers** to suppress specific recipients

Administrative control beyond your own account — creating tenants, or changing account-level
entitlements — sits with the tenant administrator.
