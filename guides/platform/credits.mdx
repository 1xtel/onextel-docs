---
title: "Credits"
description: "View credit allocation across accounts, review allocation history, and add or deduct credits from a TUC."
---

Credits are what messages are paid for with. OneXtel allocates credits to the tenant, the tenant
distributes them to each TUC, and every message sent draws down that TUC's balance.

Your available balance is shown in the portal header, split by **Web** and **API** — traffic
sent from the portal and traffic sent programmatically are metered separately.

## View allocation by account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Credits">
    In the left pane, click **Credits**. The **Userwise Allocation** tab opens by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Enter the **TUC Name** in the search box, set the credit limit, and click **Search**. The
    account's credit information is displayed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review allocation history

The **All Allocation** tab shows the full history of credit movements, which is where to look
when a balance is lower than expected.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the tab">
    Click **Credits**, then select the **All Allocation** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the period">
    Enter a **From date** and **To date** to see allocation updates for that period, then click
    **Search**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add or deduct credits

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the update">
    Click **Credits**, and on the **Userwise Allocation** tab click **+ Update Credit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the account">
    Choose the **TUC** from the dropdown. The Update button stays disabled until a TUC is
    selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the credit type">
    Select the **Credit Type** for this movement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the direction and amount">
    Select **Add-Credits** or **Deduct-Credits**, then enter the number of credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record why">
    Enter a **Remark** explaining the movement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update">
    Click **Update**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The Remark field is what makes the allocation history readable months later. A movement with no
explanation is indistinguishable from an error when you are reconciling a balance — fill it in
even for routine top-ups.
</Note>
