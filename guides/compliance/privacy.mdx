When you send a message through OneXtel, you share personal data about your recipients: their
phone numbers and the content of the message. This page explains what happens to that data.

## Where data is hosted

OneXtel runs its own data centre infrastructure in these locations:

| Country | Location |
| --- | --- |
| India | Mumbai and Noida |
| United Arab Emirates | Dubai |
| Netherlands | Amsterdam |

The data centres run in active-active or active-standby pairs. If one region goes down, another
takes over its traffic.

## Sub-processors

OneXtel uses these providers to run the service:

| Provider | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| Amazon Web Services (AWS) | Cloud hosting |
| Microsoft Azure | Cloud hosting |
| Sify and NTT | Data centre connectivity |

Messages also pass through the networks that deliver them: Meta for WhatsApp, Google and the
telecom operators for RCS, and the telecom operators and carriers for SMS.

## Retention and deletion

| Data | Kept for | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delivery reports | 3 years | Encrypted |

To have your data deleted, including when your contract ends, send OneXtel a written instruction.
OneXtel deletes or purges the data under its data purging policy, and confirms the deletion if
you ask for it.

## How personal data is handled

OneXtel's Information Security and PII Policy commits the company to:

- Process personal data lawfully, fairly and transparently.
- Collect it only for specified, explicit and legitimate purposes.
- Collect the minimum needed, and keep it only as long as necessary.
- Protect it with technical and organisational measures. See [Security](/guides/compliance/security).
- Support the rights of data subjects and respond to their requests.
- Build privacy into its processes and systems by design and by default.
- Assess privacy risks regularly and act on them.
- Train its staff on their privacy responsibilities.

## Your responsibilities

You decide who receives your messages and what they say, so some obligations stay with you:

- **Consent.** Message only the people who have agreed to hear from you on that channel. See
  opt-in and opt-out for [WhatsApp](/api-reference/whatsapp/opt-in-opt-out) and
  [RCS](/api-reference/rcs/opt-in-opt-out).
- **Content.** Keep sensitive personal data out of message bodies and template variables unless
  your own compliance team has approved it.
- **Access.** Remove portal users and API keys when people leave or integrations are retired.

## Privacy requests

For a question about personal data or a data subject request, write to OneXtel's data protection
officer at [dpo@onextel.com](mailto:dpo@onextel.com).
