| Certificate registration number | 99 310 00843 |
| Valid | 4 September 2026 to 3 September 2029, subject to annual surveillance audits |
| Sites | Noida, Pune and Bengaluru, India |

research and development, product management, sales, business development, marketing and legal.

To check that the certificate is current, search for the organisation name or the registration
number on [IAF CertSearch](https://www.iafcertsearch.org/).

## Telecom regulation in India

SMS sent to Indian numbers falls under the TRAI rules for commercial communication. OneXtel's SMS
service in India is DLT-registered, and every message is checked against your registered entity,
sender ID and template before it is sent.

You register your own entity, sender IDs and templates on a DLT platform. See
[DLT registration](/guides/sms/dlt).

## Data protection law

OneXtel's Information Security and PII Policy commits the company to the data-protection laws
that apply to it and to its customers, including the DPDP Act in India, the GDPR in the EU, and
HIPAA for healthcare data in the US.

OneXtel acts as a data controller and, where it processes data on your behalf, as a data
processor. See [Privacy](/guides/compliance/privacy) for how that data is handled.
