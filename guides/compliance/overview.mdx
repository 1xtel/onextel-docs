## At a glance

| Question | Answer | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is OneXtel ISO/IEC 27001 certified? | Yes. ISO/IEC 27001:2022, certified by TÜV SÜD, valid until 3 September 2029. | [Regulatory](/guides/compliance/regulatory#iso-27001-certificate) |

## Request evidence

The audit reports and policies are confidential documents, so they are shared on request and not
published here. Ask your OneXtel account manager, or write to
[security@onextel.com](mailto:security@onextel.com), and say which documents your review needs.

| Document | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certificate | Certification body, scope, sites, and validity dates |
| SOC 2 Type II report | The auditor's opinion and the controls tested |
| Web application penetration test report | Scope, method, findings and their retest status |
| Information Security and PII Policy | OneXtel's security and privacy commitments |
| Incident Management Policy | How incidents are detected, prioritised, contained and reviewed |
