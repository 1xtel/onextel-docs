---
title: "Schedule and launch"
description: "Split a WhatsApp or RCS campaign into batches, set up repeats, choose immediate or scheduled execution, and launch."
---

The last sections of the campaign builder control delivery: **Batching** decides how recipients
are grouped, **Repeat Campaign** sets recurring sends, and **Schedule** decides when the campaign
starts.

## Batching

Click **Batching**. The **Batching Configuration** panel shows the **Total Recipients** count, for
example 19,999, and a **Batches Preview** table that updates as you configure. Choose a method:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="All">
    Sends to every recipient in one batch. It's the default, and suits smaller campaigns or sends
    that should arrive at the same time.

    Example: 19,999 recipients → Batch 1: 19,999 recipients.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Split">
    Divides the recipients into up to 10 batches, sent at intervals, so large campaigns don't
    congest delivery.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select Split">
        Select **Split**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set the number of batches">
        Enter the **Number of Batches**, for example 3.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set the interval">
        Set the **Interval Between Batches** in `HH:MM:SS`, for example `00:30:00` for 30 minutes.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    The preview lists each batch with its recipient count and send time, for example Batch 1 –
    6,667 (2:15 PM), Batch 2 – 6,666 (2:45 PM), Batch 3 – 6,666 (3:15 PM).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Manual">
    Sets the size and send time of each batch yourself.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select Manual">
        Select **Manual**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set the batch size and time">
        Enter the **Batch Size** and set its **Schedule Time**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add more batches">
        Click **+** to add batches. The panel shows the allocated recipients against the total.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
    Manual batching locks **Repeat Campaign** and **Schedule**, because each batch already has
    its own time.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Repeat Campaign

Repeats send the same campaign on a schedule without rebuilding it. Click **Repeat Campaign**,
select **Enable Repeat**, and choose a **Frequency**:

| Frequency | What it does | How to set it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Daily** | Sends every day at the scheduled time. | Select **Daily**. The Schedule Summary shows "Every day". |
| **Weekly** | Sends on chosen days every week, on alternate weeks, or after a set number of weeks. | Choose the interval (Every, Alternate or After weeks), then the days, for example Mon, Wed, Fri. |
| **Monthly** | Sends every month on specific dates or days. | Choose the interval (Every, Alternate or After months). Under **Repeat On**, choose Specific Dates or Days of Week, then the day and Week Position, for example First Monday. |
| **Custom** | Sends on dates and times you pick. | Click **+ Add Date & Time** for each send. |

Under **Ends**, choose **Never**, **After** a number of runs, or **On** a date. Check the
**Schedule Summary** before you move on.

<Warning>
Repeat isn't available with **Immediate Execution**. Use **Scheduled Execution** when repeats are
on.
</Warning>

## Schedule

Click **Schedule** and choose the **Execution Type** under **Campaign Execution Schedule**.

### Immediate Execution

Launches the campaign as soon as it's ready.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose immediate execution">
    Select **Immediate Execution**. The panel shows the status `READY TO LAUNCH`, the execution
    mode ("As soon as campaign is launched") and the current local time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch">
    Click **Launch**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Scheduled Execution

Starts the campaign automatically at a set date, time and time zone.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose scheduled execution">
    Select **Scheduled Execution**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the time zone">
    Choose the time zone from the **Timezone** dropdown, for example `Asia/Kolkata` (IST).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date and time">
    Click the calendar icon or the **Scheduled Date & Time** field and pick when the campaign
    starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the summary">
    The **Schedule Summary** shows the time in local and UTC formats with the selected time zone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch">
    Click **Launch**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Launch controls

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Test** | Sends the campaign to a few test numbers first. Always do this before a real send. |
| **Draft** | Saves the campaign without sending. It appears on the campaigns dashboard as `Draft`. |
| **Launch** | Starts the campaign, or schedules it, per the selected execution type. |

If maker-checker is switched on for your account, **Launch** sends the campaign to a checker for
approval instead. See [Roles](/guides/platform/roles#campaign-maker-checker).

## Before you launch

- Every variable is mapped, and the preview looks right.
- Numbers include the country code, or **Default Country Code** is on.
- There is enough credit for the volume. Messages fail with `4010` when credit runs out.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="WhatsApp">
    - The template is `Approved`, and its category is the one you expect.
    - Marketing campaigns fall inside the time window set for your account.
    - Fallback is set if delivery is critical, and the template has a validity period.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="RCS">
    - The template is `Approved` for the sender profile and message type.
    - Fallback is set, so customers whose phones can't receive RCS still get the message.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## After launch

Track the campaign on the [campaigns dashboard](/guides/campaigns/manage-campaigns), where you can
pause, resume or stop it while it runs. Results are in the channel's reports:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="WhatsApp">
    Delivery, read, failure and click results are in [WhatsApp reports](/guides/whatsapp/reports).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="RCS">
    Results are in [Summary Reports](/guides/rcs-platform/reports-summary) and
    [Detail Reports](/guides/rcs-platform/reports-detail).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
