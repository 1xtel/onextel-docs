---
title: "Campaigns overview"
description: "Send bulk or targeted WhatsApp and RCS messages from one campaign builder on the OneXtel portal."
---

A campaign sends one approved template to a list of recipients, on WhatsApp or RCS. Both
channels use the same builder and the same campaigns dashboard. The pages in this section cover
them together, with **WhatsApp** and **RCS** tabs wherever the two differ.

- **WhatsApp campaigns** send approved message templates, images and documents through the
  verified WhatsApp Business API.
- **RCS campaigns** deliver rich media, interactive buttons and branded layouts to the
  customer's native messaging app.

Each campaign supports personalisation, batching, scheduling and repeats, and its results appear
in the channel's reports.

## Before you start

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="WhatsApp">
    - WhatsApp is [onboarded](/guides/whatsapp/activation) and your sender number is
      [registered](/guides/whatsapp/phone-numbers).
    - The template you want to send is [approved](/guides/whatsapp/templates).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="RCS">
    - Your [sender profile](/guides/rcs-platform/sender-profiles) is set up.
    - The template you want to send is [approved](/guides/rcs-platform/templates) for that
      profile and message type.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

You also need enough [credits](/guides/platform/credits) for the volume. To sign in and read the
main dashboard, see [Signing in](/guides/platform/getting-started).

## Build a campaign

A campaign is one form with collapsible sections. Work through them in order:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Create a campaign" href="/guides/campaigns/create-a-campaign">
    Set the title and channel, add recipients and choose the sender and template.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Variable mapping" href="/guides/campaigns/variable-mapping">
    Fill template variables with static or per-recipient values.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Additional settings" href="/guides/campaigns/additional-settings">
    Link tracking, message expiry and fallback.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Schedule and launch" href="/guides/campaigns/schedule-and-launch">
    Batching, repeat campaigns, scheduling and launch.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

To find, pause or copy a campaign after you create it, see
[Manage campaigns](/guides/campaigns/manage-campaigns).
