title: "Manage campaigns"
description: "Find, filter, copy and control WhatsApp and RCS campaigns from the campaigns dashboard."
---

The campaigns dashboard lists every WhatsApp and RCS campaign on your account. Use it to find a
campaign, check its status, and pause, stop or copy it.

## Open the campaigns dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Channels">
    In the left pane of the OneXtel portal, click **Channels**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a channel">
    Select **WhatsApp** or **RCS**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Campaigns">
    Click **Campaigns**. The campaigns dashboard (**View Campaign** page) appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the dashboard shows

Campaigns appear as cards. Each card shows the created date, channel, status, campaign name,
template, uploaded file, number of records, creator and scheduled time. A `Stopped` campaign also
shows its **Failure Reason**.

## Find a campaign

- **Search** by campaign name or campaign ID.
- **Filter** by Channel, Template, Username, Status and Date. **Clear all** resets the filters.
- **Refresh** reloads the list with the latest statuses.

The list is paginated. Use **Prev** and **Next** at the bottom of the page to move between pages.

## Campaign statuses and actions

The buttons on each card depend on the campaign's status.

| Status | Actions |
| --- | --- |
| `Created` / `Draft` (not launched) | Edit, Clone, Delete |
| `Scheduled` | View, Clone, Reschedule, Cancel |
| `Running` | View, Clone, Pause, Stop |
| `Paused` | View, Clone, Resume, Stop |
| `Completed` / `Stopped` | View, Clone |
| `Cancelled` / `Failed` | View, Clone |
| `Pending` / `Rejected` (maker-checker) | Edit, View, Clone |
| `Approved` (maker-checker) | Edit, View, Clone, Launch (checker only) |

**View** opens a read-only copy of the campaign set-up: recipients, sender, variable mapping,
settings, batching and schedule. It doesn't show results. Those are in the channel's reports:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="WhatsApp">
    [WhatsApp reports](/guides/whatsapp/reports).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="RCS">
    [Summary Reports](/guides/rcs-platform/reports-summary#report-on-one-campaign).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
The `Pending`, `Rejected` and `Approved` statuses appear only when maker-checker is switched on for
your account. A maker's campaign goes to `Pending`; a checker approves or rejects it, and launches
it once approved. See [Roles](/guides/platform/roles#campaign-maker-checker).
</Note>

## Start or copy a campaign

- **Clone Campaign** duplicates an existing campaign instead of rebuilding it.
- **+ New Campaign**, in the top right, starts a new one. See
  [Create a campaign](/guides/campaigns/create-a-campaign).
