This page covers the first sections of the campaign builder: title and channel, **Recipient
Details** and **Sender Details**. Then continue with
[variable mapping](/guides/campaigns/variable-mapping),
[additional settings](/guides/campaigns/additional-settings) and
[schedule and launch](/guides/campaigns/schedule-and-launch).

## Start a new campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Campaigns">
    In the left pane, click **Channels**, select **WhatsApp** or **RCS**, then click
    **Campaigns**. The campaigns dashboard appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the campaign">
    Click **+ New Campaign**. The New Campaign page opens with a template preview panel on the
    right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a unique campaign title">
    Enter the **Campaign Title** and click **Check**. The portal shows an alert if the title
    already exists.

    Once the title is accepted, the remaining sections (Recipient Details, Sender Details,
    Variable Mapping, Additional Settings, Batching) open up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the channel">
    Under **Select Channel**, select **WhatsApp** or **RCS**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The template preview on the right stays empty until the sender details are complete.
</Note>

## Add recipients

Click **Recipient Details**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="WhatsApp">
    First choose the **Recipient Type**:

    - **Phone Number**: the usual choice.
    - **BSUID**: Meta's business-scoped user ID, for customers who use a WhatsApp username in
      place of a visible phone number.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="RCS">
    RCS recipients are phone numbers.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Then select one of the three recipient methods:

| Method | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Copy/Paste** | Enter or paste up to 100,000 phone numbers, separated by commas or new lines, for example `919999999999, 919999999998`. Click **Save** to add them, or **Clear** to remove them. |
| **Upload** | Click **Browse** and choose a `.txt`, `.csv` or `.xls` file. In the pop-up, select the column that holds the numbers (for example `phone_number`) and click **Confirm Selection**. Extra columns can fill template variables later. |
| **Phonebook** | Choose a group from the **Phonebook Group** dropdown. Groups are created under [Phonebook](/guides/platform/phonebook). |

### Recipient options

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Default Country Code** | Adds the selected country code to every number, for example `+91` for India. Use it when your numbers don't include a country code. |
| **Remove Duplicate** | Removes repeated numbers across all files and lists, so each recipient gets the message once. On by default. |
| **Exclude Numbers** | Upload a file of numbers to leave out, such as unsubscribed or blocked contacts. |

## Configure sender details

Click **Sender Details**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="WhatsApp">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select the WABA ID">
        Select your WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) ID from the dropdown, then check the ID
        shown below it. The campaign is sent from this account.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select the template">
        Choose an approved template that matches the campaign's purpose. Templates in a category
        your administrator has blocked for your account aren't listed.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select the phone number">
        Select the sender number from the dropdown, which lists the numbers registered to the
        selected WABA ID. To send through several numbers, tick **Use number pool** and pick a
        pool instead. See [Number pools](/guides/whatsapp/phone-numbers#number-pools).
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    Templates are created and approved before the campaign. See
    [Create WhatsApp templates](/guides/whatsapp/templates).

    #### Send without a template (Direct Send)

    If Direct Send is switched on for the WABA, a **Direct Send** tick box appears in Sender
    Details.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Tick Direct Send">
        Tick **Direct Send**. The **Template** field is greyed out.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Build the message">
        Click **Configure** and build a **Text** or **Interactive** message. The body is
        required, and an interactive message needs a text or image header. Variables such as
        `{{Name}}` come from the columns of your uploaded file.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    Direct Send carries utility content only. Check the content first under **Message Sample**.
    See [Meta Direct Send](/guides/whatsapp/direct-send).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="RCS">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select the sender profile">
        Select the brand agent the campaign is sent from. To share the traffic across several
        profiles, tick **Use sender pool** and pick a
        [sender pool](/guides/rcs-platform/sender-profiles#sender-pools) instead.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select the message type">
        Select **Promotional**, **Transactional** or **OTP**, so the campaign follows the rules
        for that type.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select the template">
        Select the approved template. Only templates approved for the chosen profile and message
        type are listed.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    Templates are created and approved before the campaign. See
    [RCS templates](/guides/rcs-platform/templates).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
