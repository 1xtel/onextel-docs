OneXtel is a CPaaS (communications platform as a service) company. Businesses use OneXtel to
reach their customers on WhatsApp, RCS and SMS: one-time passwords, transaction alerts,
reminders, marketing campaigns and two-way conversations. You can send from the OneXtel portal,
from your own systems through the OneXtel APIs, or from the CRM and marketing tools your team
already uses.

## What you can do with OneXtel

| | What it is for | Start here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **WhatsApp** | Template and session messages, campaigns from the portal, and calls from customers inside WhatsApp | [About WhatsApp](/guides/channels/about-whatsapp) |
| **RCS** | Messages with your brand name, logo, images, carousels and buttons, in the phone's own messaging app | [About RCS](/guides/channels/about-rcs) |
| **SMS** | DLT-registered SMS in India, and SMS to other countries | [SMS overview](/guides/sms/overview) |
| **Fallback** | Retry a message on another channel when the first one is not delivered | [Multi-channel fallback](/guides/omnichannel/fallback-overview) |
| **Verify** | Generate, send and check one-time PINs over SMS, WhatsApp and email | [Verify API](/api-reference/verify/overview) |
| **Truecaller** | Verified business messages to Truecaller users | [Truecaller API](/api-reference/truecaller/overview) |
| **Plugins** | Send from MoEngage, CleverTap, WebEngage, Salesforce, LeadSquared, Shopify, Zoho CRM and Optimove | [Plugins and connectors](/plugins/overview) |

## Security and compliance

OneXtel's SMS service in India is DLT-registered and TRAI-compliant.

| Certification or standard | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| ISO 27001 | Information security |
| ISO 9001 | Quality management |
| ISO 22301 | Business continuity |
| SOC 2 Type II | Security and integrity |
| PCI DSS | Payment-card security |
| TL 9000 | Telecom quality |
| GDPR | EU data privacy |
| HIPAA | Healthcare data |

Security controls include AES-256 encryption, vulnerability assessment and penetration testing
(VAPT), data leakage protection, and security monitoring (SIEM).
