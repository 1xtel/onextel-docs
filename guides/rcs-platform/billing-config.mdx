---

**Channels > RCS > Configurations > Billing** shows the rates you pay for
RCS and, if you have sub-accounts, lets you set the rates they pay.

<Note>
  Billing appears only after your tenant administrator switches on Billing
  for your account. Your reports then also show a billing column for each
  message.
</Note>

## Billing message types

In OneXtel, Billing defines the charges or credits applied for sending
different types of RCS messages. There are three types of RCS messages:

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Basic** | A plain text message of up to 160 characters. |
| **Single** | One rich message: a rich card, media, or text over 160 characters. |
| **Other** | A conversation or session charge. |

### Rates per region

The billing table has one row per market — **India** and **Other** (the
rest of the world) — and one column per message type.

### Billing type tabs

- **Assigned**: The rates you pay (view only).
- **Default**: The rates all your sub-accounts pay, unless they have custom
  rates (editable).
- **Custom**: Rates for one sub-account (editable).

## Setting rates: Assigned

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Channels > RCS">
    In the left pane of the RCS platform, click **Channels**, then select
    **RCS**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Configurations > Billing">
    Click the **Configurations** tab, then click the **Billing** subtab.

    The RCS Billing details appear. By default, the **Assigned** Billing
    Type tab appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export (optional)">
    Click **Download** to export the report.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Setting rates: Default

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Configurations > Billing">
    In the left pane of the RCS platform, click **Channels**, select
    **RCS**, click the **Configurations** tab, then click the **Billing** subtab.

    The RCS Billing details appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Default">
    On the right-hand side, select the **Default** Billing Type tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search (optional)">
    Use the **Search** bar to find values. Only horizontal searches are
    supported; column-based searches are not allowed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload rates">
    Click **+Upload** to add billing rates files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit a rate">
    Double-click any value to edit the rate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply or discard changes">
    Click **Save** to save your changes. **Reset** discards unsaved edits,
    and **Reset to Assigned** copies your own Assigned rates into the
    Default tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export (optional)">
    Click **Download** to export the report.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Setting rates: Custom

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Configurations > Billing">
    In the left pane of the RCS platform, click **Channels**, select
    **RCS**, click the **Configurations** tab, then click the **Billing** subtab.

    The RCS Billing details appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Custom">
    On the right-hand side, select the **Custom** Billing Type tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search (optional)">
    Use the **Search** bar to find values. Only horizontal searches are
    supported; column-based searches are not allowed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the profile">
    Select the **Currency** and the **Profile Name** (the sub-account) from
    the dropdown lists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload rates">
    Click **+Upload** to add billing rates files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit a rate">
    Double-click any value to edit the rate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply or discard changes">
    Click **Save** to save your changes. **Reset** discards unsaved edits,
    and **Reset to Default** returns the sub-account to your Default rates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export (optional)">
    Click **Download** to export the report.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Billing flow

Rates cascade through the account hierarchy:

1. OneXtel sets the rates for your tenant administrator.
2. Your tenant administrator sets the rates you pay — your **Assigned** tab.
3. You set the rates your sub-accounts pay, on the **Default** and
   **Custom** tabs.

Any overridden rates become independent of parent changes.

## Overridden concept

If a user changes a rate in the Custom tab, it becomes an override — the
rate no longer follows parent updates. Users can also reset to default to
revert to parent rates.

<Warning>
  File upload support: only Excel (`.xlsx`) and CSV files are supported.
  These files must contain rate tables in tabular format. Word files are
  not preferred because unstructured tables can't be read correctly.
</Warning>
