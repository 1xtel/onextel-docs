<Note>
Before you write a template, check the
[template guidelines](/guides/rcs-platform/template-guidelines) — category rules, the 70% static
text requirement, and the checks that cause rejection.
</Note>
The Templates section manages predefined message formats for OneXtel RCS
communication. Templates ensure consistency, compliance, and faster
delivery of business messages. RCS templates help businesses create
structured, interactive messages that are reviewed and approved before
being sent to end-users, ensuring both quality and compliance.

<Note>
  This page covers creating and managing templates in the portal. For
  using a template inside a campaign (variable mapping, recipients,
  scheduling), see [RCS Campaigns](/guides/campaigns/overview).
</Note>

## View templates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Channels > RCS">
    In the left pane, click **Channels**, then select **RCS**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Templates">
    Click **Asset Management**, then the **Templates** subtab. Pick the
    **Profile** and **Message Type** at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export the list (optional)">
    Click **Download** to export the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Template Name** | The unique name of the template. |
| **Type** | **Text Message**, **Rich Card Stand Alone** or **Rich Card Carousel**. |
| **Status** | **Pending** (waiting for approval), **Approved** (ready to use) or **Rejected**. |

Use **Filter** to search the list. Each template's **⋮** menu has:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Clone** | Copy the template as the start of a new one |
| **Preview** | See how it looks on a phone |
| **Generate cURL** | Show a ready-made API request for this template, for your developers to copy |
| **Delete** | Remove the template |

<Note>
  RCS templates can't be edited. To change one, **Clone** it, make the
  change, and submit the copy for approval.
</Note>

## Add a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Templates">
    Go to **Channels > RCS > Asset Management > Templates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new template">
    Click **+Add Template**. The Add Template form is displayed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the approval scope">
    - **Individual**: pick the **Profile** and **Message Type**.
    - **Pool**: pick a [sender pool](/guides/rcs-platform/sender-profiles#sender-pools).
      The template is submitted for every profile in the pool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the template">
    Enter the **Template Name/Code**: letters, numbers and underscores only,
    up to 20 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow text formatting (optional)">
    Tick **Text Formatting** to allow bold, italic and strikethrough in the
    text, including in variables.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the type and fill in the content">
    See the fields below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add buttons (optional)">
    Click **+ Add Suggestion** — see [Suggested actions](#suggested-actions-and-reply-buttons).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the TTL (optional)">
    **TTL** sets how long the operator keeps trying to deliver — for example
    `45m`, `2h` or `30d`. A campaign or API call can override it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Template types

| Template type | Fields |
|---|---|
| **Text Message** | Message content, up to 2,500 characters, with variables. |
| **Rich Card Stand Alone** | **Card Orientation** (Vertical or Horizontal), **Media Height** (Short or Medium), **Media** (image, video or PDF), **Card Title** (up to 200 characters), **Card Description** (up to 2,000 characters). |
| **Rich Card Carousel** | Two or more cards. For the carousel: **Card Width** (Small or Medium) and **Media Height** (Short or Medium). For each card: media, title, description and its own buttons. |

### Media

The **Upload** button opens **Select Media**, with three sources:

- **Upload**: drag and drop or browse for a file. Unsupported image formats
  are converted to JPEG, and an animated GIF asks how to convert it.
- **Upload from URL**: a public link to the file.
- **Variable Media URL**: choose Image, Video or PDF and enter a base URL
  ending in a variable, for example `https://example.com/offers/[event]`.
  The variable is filled at send time from the campaign or API call, and the
  final link must be public.

The image rules depend on the card settings, and the builder shows them
under **Media** as you choose.

| Card | Settings | Image ratio, best size, max | Video max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stand Alone | Vertical, Short height | 3:1, 1440 × 480 px, 2 MB | 10 MB |
| Stand Alone | Vertical, Medium height | 2:1, 1440 × 720 px, 2 MB | 10 MB |
| Stand Alone | Horizontal | 3:4, 768 × 1024 px, 2 MB | 10 MB |
| Carousel | Small width, Short height | 5:4, 960 × 720 px, 1 MB | 5 MB |
| Carousel | Small width, Medium height | 4:5, 576 × 720 px, 1 MB | 5 MB |
| Carousel | Medium width, Short height | 2:1, 1440 × 720 px, 1 MB | 5 MB |
| Carousel | Medium width, Medium height | 4:3, 1440 × 1080 px, 1 MB | 5 MB |

- **PDF**: up to 10 MB on any card.
- **Formats**: images JPEG, JPG, PNG or GIF (other formats are converted);
  videos MP4 or MPEG.
- **Image fit**: **Cropper** (recommended) crops the image to the required
  ratio. **Original** sends the image as it is, and the phone crops it.
- **Video thumbnail**: JPEG, JPG or PNG, about 40 KB and no more than
  100 KB. It uses the card's ratio, except a Vertical Medium stand-alone card
  (7:3) and a Horizontal card (25:33).

<Warning>
  If you change the orientation, width or height after uploading, check the
  image still fits the new ratio. The builder reminds you, but a wrongly
  sized image is cropped on the phone.
</Warning>

### Suggested actions and reply buttons

Each suggestion has a **Suggestion Text** (the button label, up to 25
characters) and a **Suggestion Postback** (up to 120 characters), which is
sent back to you when the customer taps it. Use **Add Variable** to put
variables in either.

| Type of action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Reply** | Sends a quick reply back to you. |
| **Dialer Action** | Opens the phone dialler with a number. |
| **URL Action** | Opens a web link. **Application** sets how: **Browser**, or in-app **Webview-Full**, **Webview-Half** or **Webview-Tall**. |
| **Dynamic Link Tracking** | A short tracked link (domain plus variable), so clicks show in URL reports. |
| **View Location (Lat/Long)** / **View Location (Query)** | Opens a map pin. |
| **Share Location** | Asks the customer to share their location. |
| **Calendar Event** | Adds an event to the customer's calendar. |

## Submit for approval

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit for Approval**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the confirmation pop-up, mark the checkbox and click **Submit**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Who approves the template depends on the operator of the sender profile:

| Operator | Approval |
|---|---|
| **Vi** | The template goes straight to Vi for approval. |
| **Jio** | The template waits as **Pending** until your tenant administrator approves it. If Template Auto Approval is switched on for your account, it is approved automatically within about 2 minutes. |

Before you submit, check the content rules in the
[template guidelines](/guides/rcs-platform/template-guidelines): the brand
name in the body, at least 70% fixed text, no two variables side by side,
and the message type that matches the content.
